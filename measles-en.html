Measles Vaccine

The measles vaccine is one of the most important childhood vaccines because measles is extremely contagious and can cause serious complications.

Quick Research Card

AgentMeasles virus
Vaccine typeLive attenuated
Common formMMR or MMRV
Main challengeVery high contagiousness

Medical Background

Measles causes fever, cough, runny nose, eye inflammation, and rash. It is sometimes wrongly seen as a mild childhood disease, but it can cause pneumonia, encephalitis, hospitalization, and death.

Public Health Importance

Because measles spreads so easily, high vaccine coverage is essential. The vaccine protects individuals and also helps protect infants and immunocompromised people who cannot receive a live vaccine.

Etymology

The word measles is probably related to Germanic words describing small spots or skin lesions.
