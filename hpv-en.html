HPV Vaccine

The HPV vaccine is one of the major cancer-prevention vaccines, especially for cervical cancer and other diseases associated with human papillomavirus.

Quick Research Card

AgentHuman papillomavirus
Vaccine typeVirus-like particles
Main fieldInfection and cancer prevention
TechnologyVirus-like shell without active genetic material

Medical Background

HPV is a large group of viruses. Some types cause warts, while others are associated with cancer. Persistent infection with high-risk types is a major risk factor for cervical cancer and other cancers.

Technology

The vaccine uses virus-like particles that resemble the outer structure of the virus but contain no active viral genetic material and cannot cause infection.

Etymology

HPV stands for Human Papillomavirus. Papilloma refers to a nipple-like or wart-like growth.

Key point: HPV is a clear example of a cancer-prevention vaccine.
