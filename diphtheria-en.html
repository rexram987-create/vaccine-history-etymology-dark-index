Diphtheria Vaccine

The diphtheria vaccine is a classic toxoid vaccine: the immune system does not need to recognize the whole bacterium, but the dangerous toxin it produces.

Quick Research Card

AgentCorynebacterium diphtheriae
Vaccine typeToxoid
Main damageThroat, heart, nervous system
Core ideaNeutralizing a toxin

Medical Background

Diphtheria can form a thick membrane in the throat and can damage distant organs through a bacterial toxin, especially the heart and nervous system.

Toxoid Principle

A toxoid is a toxin that has been inactivated so it cannot cause disease, while still teaching the immune system to make neutralizing antibodies.

Etymology

Diphtheria comes from Greek diphthera, meaning leather, hide, or membrane, referring to the membrane that can appear in the throat.

Key point: diphtheria teaches that sometimes the target is the toxin, not the whole bacterium.
