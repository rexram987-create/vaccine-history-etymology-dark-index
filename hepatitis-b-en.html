Hepatitis B Vaccine

The hepatitis B vaccine is a milestone in recombinant vaccine technology and is also connected to the prevention of liver cancer caused by chronic HBV infection.

Medical Background

Hepatitis B is a virus that affects the liver. Chronic infection can lead over many years to chronic hepatitis, cirrhosis, liver failure, and liver cancer.

Recombinant Vaccine

The vaccine presents a defined surface protein of the virus, produced by recombinant technology. It does not contain an active whole virus.

Etymology

Hepatitis combines hepar/hepat, meaning liver, and -itis, meaning inflammation. The letter B identifies one of the viral types of hepatitis.

Key point: hepatitis B vaccination prevents infection and also helps prevent liver cancer.
