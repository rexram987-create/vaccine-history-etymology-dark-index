Short Historical Distinction

Jenner is regarded as the father of modern vaccination because of smallpox vaccination. Pasteur did not invent vaccination, but transformed it into a laboratory-based scientific method. Calmette developed antivenom serum; that is ready-made antibody therapy, not ordinary active vaccination.
